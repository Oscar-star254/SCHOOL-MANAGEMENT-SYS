# EduNest user manual outline

1. Getting started: login, password recovery, school switcher, profile and theme.
2. School setup: identity, brand, academic years, terms, subjects, grading and roles.
3. Student lifecycle: admission, guardians, profiles, transfers, promotion and graduation.
4. Teaching and learning: classes, timetables, attendance, assessments, marks and report cards.
5. Fees: structures, invoices, aid, payment entry, M-Pesa reconciliation, receipts and statements.
6. Communication: templates, recipient groups, scheduling and delivery reports.
7. Parent, student and teacher portals.
8. Admissions, library, transport, boarding, meals, inventory, health and activities.
9. Staff, leave, payroll, finance and analytics.
10. Security: permissions, audit history, exports, retention and incident reporting.
11. Platform administration: schools, plans, support, feature access and impersonation controls.
12. Troubleshooting and support escalation.
